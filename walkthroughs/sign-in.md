# Sign in to Markup AI

Markup AI Lint needs a Markup AI account to check your content.

Two ways to sign in:

- **Browser** — opens markup.ai in your default browser and signs you in with your usual account.
- **Token** — paste an access token (JWT) or a `mat_…` API key, useful for CI or when a token was issued elsewhere.

You can always find sign-in again in:

- the **status bar** item at the bottom right (`MarkupAI: Sign in`),
- the **Markup AI Lint** icon in the activity bar,
- the Command Palette: **Markup AI Lint: Sign In**.
