# Check a document

Open a supported file — Markdown, HTML, DITA, XML, or plain text — and run **MarkupAI - Check Content** (or just open the file: checks run automatically on open by default).

After a check:

- Issues are underlined **inline** in the editor, with quick fixes on hover.
- The **Findings** panel lists every issue; click one to jump to it.
- The **status bar** shows the document's quality score.
- The **Folder Scanner** in the activity bar can check whole folders at once.
